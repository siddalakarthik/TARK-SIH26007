# Technical scope of the revised film

The project is presented confidently as an integrated software engineering demonstrator, not a physically commissioned mining vehicle. A targeted review found no new physical-construction or physical-validation evidence. The existing software freeze and automated verification records support the integration claim. The corrected electrical design remains pre-hardware with recorded HOLDs.

The revision removes repeated pending-status narration, not the distinction between design and physical proof. The conceptual rover/mine artwork remains labelled. Genuine public-dashboard stills from 22 September 2026 retain their own simulation, unavailable and traction-disabled labels. They are not live hardware footage. No application code, safety logic, electrical topology or actual UI data was changed.

## Specific technical boundaries

- Radar supplies the current decision pipeline. Camera, thermal and IMU interfaces provide observations and health; no completed multisensor fusion or RGB detection claim is made.
- A TTC helper exists, but the current runtime does not call it; the actual dashboard displays N/A. The film explains the concept without portraying it as an active decision input.
- The stopping expression is parameterized, not a measured braking result. Observable range is compared against the modeled requirement.
- The implemented runtime states shown are NORMAL, RESTRICT, STOP and UNKNOWN. WARN is not invented as a demonstrated transition.
- Pi transport gating and ESP32 command validation are software boundaries. Board-specific USB/PWM/watchdog binding and physical motor operation are not claimed demonstrated.
- The independent E-stop is an electrical design. No claim of a tested physical stop, resolved purchased-terminal details or energization permission is made.
- GNSS supplies observational map context. Browser location is separate; radar X/Y remain vehicle-local. No physical fix or fabricated address is claimed.
- The demonstration has zero motion authority. No claim of mine certification, deployment approval, completed physical validation or defect-free software is made.
