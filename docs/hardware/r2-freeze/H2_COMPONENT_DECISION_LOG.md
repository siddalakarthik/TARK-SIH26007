## Cost-sensitive decision gate

The priced subset is INR 184,449.64. With INR 45,000 integration reserves it reaches INR 229,449.64, excluding unpriced adapter/cables and unresolved landed charges. The assessed flagship configuration therefore **does not survive the current budget gate**. A lower exact-SKU compute quote or documented loan may be researched; neither is assumed. Do not remove essential protection or testing to force arithmetic under the ceiling.

An architecture tradeoff may be reopened only with its requirement, lost capability, interface and cost impact recorded as a new CR-A entry. This log does not authorize a weaker silent substitution or defer mandatory hardware selection into a supposedly passed Batch B.
