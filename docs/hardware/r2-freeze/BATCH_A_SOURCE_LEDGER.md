## Source-integrity decisions

1. No scraped/search-index stock value becomes a delivery promise. S03 is retained as an attributed low-confidence price observation, not accepted procurement evidence.
2. Failed manufacturer page access leaves board-level fields **UNKNOWN**, not 'NOT PUBLISHED'. The latter is used only when a reviewed source omits that field.
3. Variant or price conflicts require a dated exact-part quotation/photograph. They are not solved by averaging prices or choosing a favorable older entry.
4. Landed cost remains unavailable where tax, freight, import or delivery terms are missing. Reserve amounts are engineering allowances only.
5. No manufacturer range, TOPS, thermal sensitivity or RTK marketing number is a measured TARK result.
