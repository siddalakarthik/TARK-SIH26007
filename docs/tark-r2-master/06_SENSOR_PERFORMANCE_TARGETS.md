## Derivations to use later, with units and assumptions

- Radar range resolution: `delta_R = c / (2 B)` for the configured bandwidth B. Resolution is not range accuracy, detection probability or maximum usable range.
- Wheel response: `v_wheel = r * delta_theta / delta_t` after calibrated angle unwrapping. `r` must be measured. Direction comes from signed angle change; invalid samples invalidate the estimate. The maximum possible angle advance between valid samples must remain below the unwrap ambiguity boundary.
- Simple constant-speed TTC: `TTC = supported_longitudinal_gap / positive_closing_speed` only when geometry/association support that projection. Radial velocity cannot be substituted indiscriminately; receding/uncertain motion yields no valid TTC.
- Illustrative stopping requirement: `D_req = v * latency + v^2 / (2 * a_effective) + margin`, with `a_effective > 0`. This is a modelling relation, not a change to current safety logic. Slope, load, adhesion and brake dynamics require validated industrial parameters; unknown evidence cannot justify speed.
- RTK map matching must combine position covariance/quality, antenna-to-reference offset, map error and time age. An uncertainty region crossing multiple roads prevents a confident road assignment.
- Cooperative conflict uses **arrival-time intervals** at a shared route region, not just Euclidean separation or a single optimistic ETA. Nonparticipating vehicles remain local-sensing risks.

## Controlled artificial-aerosol low-visibility experiment

Reserve matched clear/low-contrast trials, repeated placements, fixed mounts and logged acquisition settings. Use printed high-contrast reference targets at measured distances with fixed illumination and documented observer/camera conditions. Record the furthest reference still resolved and the method's limitations. If exposure/illumination cannot be held or measured consistently, do not convert brightness changes into visibility metres.

This method provides a comparative experimental condition, **not calibrated meteorological optical range**. Relative humidity alone is not visibility. Use purpose-made equipment/fluid according to manufacturer safety instructions in a permitted, ventilated test area; no people in vehicle paths, no improvised smoke, no unattended enclosed exposure. Borrowing/rental of aerosol equipment and reference instruments is a disclosed budget assumption, not an assertion the team already owns them. Weather, droplet spectrum, wind, road spray and real mine dust require later controlled field work.
