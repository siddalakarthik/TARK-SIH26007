# 33 — Expected impact and measurable benefits

These are mechanisms and evaluation plans, not measured productivity gains.

| SIH outcome | Mechanism | KPI | Prototype evidence | Industrial validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Safety | Earlier qualified hazard/conflict warnings, honest unknown states | Missed/false warnings, useful lead time, driver comprehension | Controlled target/fault/reference runs | Exposure-adjusted incidents/conflicts and independent safety review |
| Efficiency | Route/closure awareness and fewer uninformed interruptions | Comparable route completion time, unnecessary-warning delay | Matched small-course tasks | Loaded/empty haul cycle with weather/load/queue controls |
| Fleet utilization | Health/incident visibility and managed restrictions | Available/restricted/down time with cause | Actual logged intervals | Shift-level productive utilization and maintenance data |
| Continuity | Independent local sensing through nonlocal failures | Local availability and explicitly lost cooperative capability | Network/browser/base loss runs | Representative monsoon traffic/site availability |
| Control-room awareness | Common map, stale-node state and evidence replay | Event reconstruction completeness and decision time | Two physical nodes plus labelled simulations | Multi-shift operator workload and response evaluation |
| Production-loss reduction | Potential reduction in avoidable delay under equal safety constraints | Tonnes/shift and ore evacuation, normalized for confounders | No direct production measurement possible | Controlled operational baseline/pilot |
| Scalability | Versioned nodes, bounded transport and local processing | Load/latency/storage growth with declared N | Measured simulated load; no fake fleet | Site-wide RF/maintenance/security/cost evaluation |

Cycle time = loading + loaded travel + dump queue + dumping + empty travel + load queue. Availability needs a predeclared planned-time/downtime denominator. Relative change=(baseline−assisted)/baseline only for comparable cohorts with uncertainty; an altered workload cannot substantiate improvement.

Report negative/null findings. Faster simulation, a larger supported-speed number or fewer logged alerts alone is not evidence of safer productivity. No accident-prevention count, monsoon immunity or percentage production improvement is asserted.
