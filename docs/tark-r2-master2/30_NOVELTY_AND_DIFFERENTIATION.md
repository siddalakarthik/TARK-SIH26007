# 30 — Novelty and differentiation

[Master-1 competitor matrix](../tark-r2-master/01_COMPETITOR_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) records accessible earlier title/description evidence; ten videos were inaccessible. Unknown competitor capability is not absence. Radar/thermal/GPS/dashboard/stop alone are overlapping ideas, not exclusive novelty.

| Common baseline | TARK addition | Why / hardware support | Software support to implement | Demonstration evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Live sensor display | Origin/time/calibration/validity provenance | Identifiable sensors and NVMe enable traceability | Evidence envelope/health/time | Delay/reboot source; show invalidation and recorded cause |
| Multimodal sensor list | Retained disagreement and unmatched hypotheses | Radar/RGB/thermal are complementary, not redundant truth | Gated late association | Radar-only/RGB-only/thermal-only cases without forced class/range |
| Fixed distance alarm | Uncertainty/coverage-governed envelope | Characterized geometry and motion evidence | Stopping/coverage/capability model | More uncertainty never grants more capability |
| GPS marker | Quality-aware mine edge/maneuver guidance | Two rovers/base plus IMU | Covariance/map matching/local graph | Adjacent-road ambiguity suppresses turn |
| Vehicle proximity | Route-region occupancy intervals | Physical peer, reference GNSS and AP | Blind-curve conflict engine | Opposing unseen nodes plus stale-node challenge |
| Dashboard history | Evidence-linked incident reconstruction | NVMe/clocked sources | Versioned recording/checkpoints | Original and recomputed state/reason comparison |
| Cloud monitoring | Independent local decision path | Jetson/local sensors/ESP | Observer-independent lifecycle | Internet/browser/AP loss with distinct capability effects |
| Healthy/fault badge | Explicit remaining/lost capabilities | Separate source health | State/dependency policy | One fault cannot silently increase authority |
| Attractive demo | Predeclared failure and low-visibility experiment | Calibration/aerosol budget | Run manifests and falsifiable KPIs | Report misses and limits alongside successes |

These are design differentiators to demonstrate, not patentability/world-first conclusions or proof competitors lack them. Strength comes from reproducible behavior and honest limits. No percentage “better” score or fabricated comparative ranking is used.
