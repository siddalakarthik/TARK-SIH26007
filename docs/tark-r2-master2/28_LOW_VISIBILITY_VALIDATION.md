# 28 — Controlled artificial-aerosol low-visibility experiment

This exact name distinguishes the experiment from real mine validation. Do not call it Bailadila fog recreation.

Use a permitted controlled area or enclosure designed for the equipment, with competent supervision, ventilation, restricted access and manufacturer-approved fluid/equipment. No improvised smoke/combustion, unprotected mains/liquid contact or people in a motor path. Traction remains disabled until separate commissioning approval. Stop for ventilation failure, liquid ingress, overheating or obscured escape routes.

## Layout and references

Measure target distances/angles and mount transforms. Use an RGB contrast chart, characterized radar reflector/targets and safe thermal reference whose actual contrast is recorded. Keep cables away from liquid/traffic, electronics protected without obstructing RF/LWIR or cooling. Provide spill/isolation arrangements suited to the venue/equipment.

Select baseline clear air, several repeatable aerosol-condition bins, and recovery clear air. Record fluid/equipment settings, settling time, illumination/exposure, ambient conditions where actually measured, target identity/orientation, reference method and acquisition loss. Aerosol bins are experimental conditions, not invented visibility-meter readings.

A practical optical reference uses chart contrast at known distances under controlled illumination/exposure. Record Michelson contrast (I_max−I_min)/(I_max+I_min) with clipping/noise guards, chart ROI and reference setup. Auto exposure or illumination drift can invalidate comparisons. If an appropriate calibrated transmission instrument is borrowed, record its uncertainty and path; otherwise do not report meteorological optical range. Relative humidity alone is not visibility.

## Trial design

1. Calibrate/validate each sensor in clear air; establish recording and reference coverage.
2. Randomize or counterbalance condition/target order where feasible; fix geometry and settings.
3. Collect repeated independent placements/trajectories per condition, not thousands of adjacent frames as independent trials.
4. Compare RGB quality/detection, radar detection/track residuals, thermal region continuity and cross-sensor association. Preserve misses/false alarms and unsupported conditions.
5. Inject one documented source loss/stale condition at a time and inspect capability/state/reason.
6. Restore clear air and verify recovery/remount stability; do not claim uninterrupted support through invalid periods.

Primary outputs: per-condition detection/miss/false-alarm counts, localization/reference scope, warning lead time, evidence age, frame/track continuity, state changes and replay completeness. Record latency distributions and uncertainty. No chosen range, fog attenuation, full-weather reliability or human-detection guarantee is inferred from a single successful scene.

No experiment is performed by this document. Actual outcomes may require narrowing the operating domain; failed targets must remain in the report.
